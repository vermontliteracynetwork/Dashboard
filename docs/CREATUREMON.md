# Creaturemon: the running creature table

Every creature in Creaturemon, one row per evolution (every evolution has its own card). Kept up to date from `src/games/creaturemon/roster.json` by `node scripts/creaturemon-table.mjs`; the PDF version with pictures is `docs/creaturemon-table.pdf`.

**61 creatures in 20 evolution lines.** Names are built from each creature's body shape, in the style of the classic creature games (two words blended, the way the classics name theirs). Stats are at level 1; every level adds 4%.

Types and what they beat (special moves only; a strong hit does 1.5 times the damage, a weak one 0.7): Fire beats Leaf and Frost. Water beats Fire and Stone. Leaf beats Water and Stone. Frost beats Leaf and Sky. Stone beats Fire and Spark. Sky beats Leaf and Spark. Spark beats Water and Sky. Shadow and Light beat each other.

## Abilities (each creature, up to 3 of its own)

Lazy: naps in battle to get health back. Spiky: anyone who hits it gets poked back. Speedy: dodges more. Brave: hits harder with low hearts. Lucky: sometimes a double hit. Tough: takes less from every hit. Cheerful: more XP. Focused: moves fueled by right answers hit harder. Zen: Guard blocks more (from meditating). Snacky: berries heal more. Learned by winning battles, by every evolution and by meditating.
