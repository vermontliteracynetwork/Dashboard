Plinko: development plan

Build Queue 243 to 246, 2026-10-20. Modeled on Baamboozle's Plink-oh, built our way: questions first, iPad first, read aloud, no em dashes.

Her words: "1 - the design of our plink needs to be modeled after Baamboozles Plink-oh game. 2, questions should alwyas pop up in our native regular way, drop zones selected after. power ups should show on the right side, score board on the left. 3- multiplayer game." "I want to make this game with customizable theme/designs ... a pdf file (or image files) that can be downloaded, uploaded to canva, and added ... they can then be selected at the main menu screen after i upload the theme as a teacher."

Where it is today

  • Plinko Drop: one player. The four answers label four chutes at the top; dropping down the right chute sends the ball through the pegs into a ticket slot (2, 5, 1, 3, 10, 3, 1, 5, 2). Tickets buy carnival plushies at the Prize Counter; $1 Class Cash per right answer.
  • Start card with a "Drops this game" slider (5 to 20).
  • What changes: the answer is no longer the drop chute. The question comes first, in the normal question pop-up, and only a right answer earns a drop.

The new game, screen by screen

1. Main menu (Plink-oh setup screen)

  • Big themed title (the theme's title image), three big sliders: Players (1 to 4), Questions (how many drops in the whole game, shown as "8 each"), Game time (off, 3, 5, 10 minutes).
  • Theme picker: a row of theme covers (Carnival, Winter, Dogs, and every theme she uploads). The picked theme dresses every screen.
  • Next goes to Players. Same settings card rules as every game (her assignment can lock them).

2. Players

  • One big ball per player, each in its theme ball picture, with a name button: the student's own name for player 1, then classmates typed or picked, or a Neighbor (Phase 2). Random shuffles who goes first.

3. A turn

  • Question first: the native question pop-up (the same one every game uses: read aloud, the 3-second read first pause, toolbox, one try).
  • Right answer: the drop board opens. The ball slides along the top; the student taps a drop zone (or drags the ball and lets go). Plink-oh style board: tall, about 6 columns of pegs, a few bonus pegs, four big score buckets at the bottom (10, 30, 20, 40, order shuffled every round).
  • Wrong answer: the right answer shows and the turn passes (Skip, like Plink-oh). No drop, no points.
  • Left side: scoreboard. Every player's ball, name and score, a crown on the leader, "Round 2 of 8" at the bottom.
  • Right side: power-ups. A card shows the power-up this turn earned (about one in three right answers), with Use and Save buttons.

4. Power-ups (first set)

Power-upWhat it does
DoubleThis drop's bucket counts twice.
Big BallA bigger ball that bounces less, easier to aim.
MagnetPulls the ball toward the 40 bucket.
Second DropDrop a second ball.
SwapTrade scores with the leader (multiplayer only, once a game).
FreezeThe next player's buckets are all 10 (multiplayer only).

Bonus pegs on the board (like Plink-oh's stars): touching one adds 5 points. Nothing is ever taken away from a student for a wrong answer.

5. End

  • Podium with every player's ball and score, confetti, the winner read aloud. Class Cash goes to the logged-in student ($1 per right answer, as today). Tickets and the Prize Counter stay.

Custom themes: how it works (updated with her rules, Build Queue 251)

What a theme can change

PartWhat you design
BackgroundFully your design.
Board and pegsColors only: border, background, pegs. Peg placement never changes.
BallsYour ball designs: at least 5 (one per player), up to 10.
BucketsColors only (up to 8).
TitleThe game name in your lettering, text only (see-through background).
Side panelsBackground design, plus the theme font.
Power-upsButton and background colors.
Palette and fontPrimary (main color), secondary (buttons), text color; the font is picked from a list when uploading.

Making it (one Canva project)

  • Download plinko-theme-template.pdf: 5 pages, all the same size, so it opens in Canva as one project (Create a design, Import file).
  • Everything solid black is off limits; white words on the black say what each white space is for. There are no words inside the white spaces.
  • Page 1 Background (white box). Page 2 Balls (10 white circles, 1 to 5 needed). Page 3 Title (letters only; the white around them turns see-through). Page 4 Side panel. Page 5 Colors (fill each white square with one solid color: primary, secondary, text, board border, board background, pegs, power-up background, power-up buttons, buckets 1 to 8).
  • Download the whole project as one PDF (Share, Download, PDF Standard) and upload that one file.

Adding it (teacher)

  • Teacher → Plinko themes → Upload: pick the PDF, name the theme, choose the font. The app cuts out every piece, reads the colors and shows the real board as a preview.
  • New themes start hidden; only Approve puts a theme on the students' theme picker. Until then everyone plays the plain default look. Students who design a theme hand her the PDF.

Claudia's checks (high support learners)

  • Read first: questions come before any aiming, so a student cannot win by tapping fast. The 3-second pause applies.
  • Aiming is easy for every motor level: tap a drop zone (no precise drag needed), Big Ball helps.
  • Turn taking is clear: whose turn it is shows in the player's ball color and is read aloud.
  • Themes must keep the ball and buckets easy to see: the preview warns when the board is too busy or text is hard to read.
  • iPad first: big buttons, both orientations (in portrait the scoreboard and power-ups sit above and below the board).
