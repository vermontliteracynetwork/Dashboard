Plinko Theme Kit

Everything a student or teacher needs to design a Plinko theme, on paper or in Canva.

What is in the kit

  • Plinko-Theme-Template.pdf: the whole template, 20 pages, 11 x 8.5 in. Use it for printing and for Canva. Every page is one flat picture with no text boxes or shapes, so nothing on it can be edited or pulled apart.
  • single-pages: the same pages as pictures, if you only need one page.
  • main-theme-reference: the colors and font of the built-in Plinko look.

Pages

1 Theme Cover Image (needed), 2 Background, 3 Title, 4 Game Board, 5 Score Board Background, 6 to 15 Balls 1 to 10 (balls 1 to 5 needed), 16 to 18 Power-up Cover Images (Magnet, Double, Big Ball), 19 Double Ball Drop (its cover image and both balls), 20 New Power-up Cover Image (copy it once for each new power-up; each copy you upload adds one, and its name and action are set in the theme builder). Colors and fonts are picked in the theme builder, not on paper. Leave anything white to keep the usual look.

On paper

  1. Print Plinko-Theme-Template.pdf at actual size (or only the pages you need).
  2. Draw and color only inside the outlined shapes. Keep the four black corner squares and the small page-number squares clear.

In Canva

  1. In Canva, tap Upload, then upload Plinko-Theme-Template.pdf. Canva opens it as one design with all 20 pages, already 11 x 8.5 in.
  2. On each page, tap the template picture, then right click (or the three dots) and choose Set image as background. It becomes the page background: it cannot be moved, resized or picked by mistake while designing.
  3. For extra safety you can also tap the lock icon (Lock) on the page.
  4. Design on top, only inside the outlined shapes.
  5. When finished: Share, Download, PDF Standard, all pages.

Why not a locked PDF file? Canva will not open a password or permission-locked PDF, so the template is flattened instead (each page is one solid picture) and the lock happens inside Canva with Set image as background.

Upload the finished theme

  1. Teacher dashboard: Games, Themes, Plinko.
  2. In the Template bar, upload the PDF, pictures of the pages, or Take photos with the iPad. Pages can come in any order: each page reads its own number. Get the whole page and all four corner squares in the photo.
  3. To redo one part, use the Scan button on that card.
  4. Check the preview, pick colors and fonts, then Publish.
