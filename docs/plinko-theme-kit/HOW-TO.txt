Plinko custom theme kit

The template is 7 pages, each 11 x 8.5 in (landscape letter).
Draw or design only inside the dashed lines. The gray striped area is outside.
Keep the four black corner squares clear. They tell the app which way is up.

Pages
1 Cover (needed)       2 Background        3 Balls (1 to 5 needed, 6 to 10 extra)
4 Title (the white around the letters turns see-through)
5 Board and side panel 6 Colors (one solid color per square)
7 Power-ups (a picture for each, they keep the same action)
Leave anything white to keep the usual look.

On paper
1. Print plinko-theme-template.pdf (or the page PNGs) at actual size.
2. Draw and color inside the dashed shapes.

In Canva
1. Make an 11 x 8.5 in design with 7 pages.
2. Upload page-1.png to page-7.png, put each one on its page full size,
   then right click it, Lock, so it cannot move.
3. Design on top inside the shapes. Download as PDF Standard, all 7 pages.

Upload (teacher dashboard: Games, Themes, Plinko)
- Upload the PDF, or pictures of the pages, or Take photos with the iPad.
  Pictures go in page order. Get the whole page and all four corners in the photo.
- To redo one part, tap Scan page N on that card and upload just that page.
- Check the preview, then turn on Show to students.
